Welcome to NimbusOS
===================

Tips:
 * Double-click an icon on the desktop to open it.
 * Drag icons to rearrange them; positions are remembered.
 * Right-click the desktop for New Folder, New Text File,
   Change Wallpaper, Refresh and Properties.
 * Alt+Tab cycles between windows, the Win key opens Start.
 * Drag window edges or corners to resize, double-click a
   title bar to maximize.

Have fun!
